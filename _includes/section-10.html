Frequently asked

What does AI automation cost for a small business?

Our three products range from $600–$1,200 one-time to set up, with monthly retainers from $150 to $450 depending on the system. Bundle two or three products and the setup fee drops by 15–25%. You'll always see the full number before we start.

How long does setup take?

Most systems go from first call to running in 2 to 3 weeks. Simple workflows can be live within days.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We build everything, and nothing changes in how you work day to day. You keep using your normal inbox and tools; they just start working for you.

What happens to my data? Is this GDPR-compliant?

Your data stays in your own accounts. We work with a signed data processing agreement with our AI provider and document exactly what flows where before anything goes live, in writing.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

Where a message goes out automatically without you reviewing it first, like an instant lead reply or a review request, we add a simple line disclosing it's automated, in line with EU AI Act transparency rules. Anything you personally review and send, like a drafted email reply, doesn't need this since you're the one sending it.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

The systems are built so a human stays in control: replies are drafted for your review, not sent blindly, and we monitor every workflow. When something looks off, we fix it, usually before you notice.

When will it be finished?

Already done. That's the point: by the time you're asking, the work happened overnight. (For new setups: 2 to 3 weeks from the first call.)
